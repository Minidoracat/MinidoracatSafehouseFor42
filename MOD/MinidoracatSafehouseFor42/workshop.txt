description=[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Required:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3789836701]Minidoracat UI Library[/url]
description=[*] [b]Optional:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3763913359]Minidoracat MiniMap[/url] (keep claims off resource points)
description=[*] [b]Optional:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3801482125]Minidoracat Economy[/url] (buy or rent extra safehouse slots with in-game money, if the server sells them)
description=[*] Dedicated multiplayer servers only; the server admin must change a few vanilla safehouse settings first (see the full guide)
description=[*] [b]Add/remove mid-save:[/b] can be added mid-save; safehouses already in the save are taken over automatically when the server starts. Read the full guide before removing it: deeds disappear and existing safehouses can no longer be managed
description=[*] [b]Languages:[/b] 繁體中文, 简体中文, English, 日本語, 한국어, Русский, Español, Português, Türkçe, Français, Polski, Deutsch (please report any translation issues)
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Craft a safehouse deed (the server may also sell deeds in the shop, let them drop as loot or allow free claims)
description=[*] Right-click the deed, or open Safehouse Manager from the family toolbar, and choose to create a safehouse
description=[*] Drag with the mouse or walk to two corners to mark the area, then confirm while standing inside it
description=[*] Share the safehouse with friends or your faction from the manager window
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Tiered deeds[/b]: the deed tier sets how large your safehouse can be
description=[*] [b]Custom area[/b]: drag or walk to two corners to mark the area, and join pieces into L or U shapes
description=[*] [b]Avoids roads and resource points[/b]: roads, parking lots and buildings such as military, police and medical sites cannot be claimed
description=[*] [b]Per-permission sharing[/b]: share with players or your faction, granting appliances, building and farming separately
description=[*] [b]Manager window[/b]: rename, share or release your safehouses, set a respawn point, and see nearby safehouses outlined on the ground
description=[*] [b]Vanilla protection[/b]: non-members cannot enter or take items, but zombies can still spawn inside
description=[*] [b]Admin panel[/b]: adjust every rule in game, including deeds, exclusions and paid slots
description=[*] [b]Idle release[/b]: safehouses nobody visits for too long are released; owners can buy insurance to keep them longer
description=[/list]
